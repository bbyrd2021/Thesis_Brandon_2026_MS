CA-07 | Medium, attribution/derivation clarification | Thesis §3.12 physicalPDF p52 (printed39), Eq3.15; ch3_methodology.tex lines412–421.
No citation appears beside this multi-positive contrastive objective, and the thesis does not identify whether its formula was adapted from a particular prior implementation or independently assembled from standard contrastive/cross-entropy operations. Add relevant methodological credit and expressly identify the local objective. A technically accurate description is a supervised visual-to-phrase, multi-positive softmax objective, averaging log probabilities over the positive label phrases. It is equivalently cross-entropy against a target distribution uniform on the positive labels.
Khosla et al. SupCon Eq2 is a relevant multiple-positive contrastive foundation, but is NOT this exact objective: its positives/denominator are other augmented visual examples in a batch, whereas the thesis uses184 adapted label phrases and excludes all-zero rows. Likewise this is NOT the symmetric in-batch image/text CLIP pretraining loss: it is one-directional box-to-class-phrase supervision. The thesis should not label it an exact reproduction of SupCon or CLIP, nor imply the generic multi-positive averaging is new. Cite the actual inspiration/implementation if known; cite SupCon/CLIP as related foundations only if the text makes that relationship explicit. Source comparison alone cannot prove historical derivation.
Original source checked: https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2020/file/d89a66c7c80a29b1bdbab0f2a1a94af8-Paper.pdf (Khosla et al., Supervised Contrastive Learning, NeurIPS2020, Eq2).
Existing CLIP source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/radford21a.html .
