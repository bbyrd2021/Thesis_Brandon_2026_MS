\section*{Author decisions still open}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Training-recipe scope (Section 3.4).} The challenge report is now cited and the broad ``follows'' wording is narrowed to ``informed by.'' Verify which settings were actually borrowed. The report's full system adds multi-branch/dual-stream processing and other changes beyond the thesis's standard YOLOv8x detector. The citation does not establish reproduction of that entire pipeline.
\item \textbf{Contrastive objective (Section 3.12).} Identify the actual inspiration or implementation for Eq. 3.15. The visual-to-label-phrase objective is not an exact reproduction of symmetric CLIP or sample-to-sample SupCon. No invented derivation or unsupported citation was inserted.
\item \textbf{Figure provenance.} Method credits were added to the 3D-RetinaNet, YOLOv8x, RoIAlign and InternVideo2 captions. Confirm whether these diagrams/components are original drawings, adapted or reproduced, and document upstream assets/terms as applicable. Figure 12's composition-detail asset still traces only to an older deck. The added method references do not settle image authorship or reuse permission.
\item \textbf{Checkpoint provenance (Section 3.15).} The official scaled checkpoint and text-model configuration are now cited. Recover the actual cached revision/checkpoint hashes and text-model provenance from launch records where available; no historical revision has been invented.
\item \textbf{Interpretation limits.} Retain the existing qualifications on phrase shuffling, validation reuse, noncausal context and external benchmark protocols. Same-objective ensemble controls and a full independent training/evaluation rerun were not established by this audit.
\item \textbf{Slide follow-through.} The linked thesis repository does not contain the current defense deck. Slide 7's PIE total should be 909,480 video frames; 740,901 counts pedestrian boxes. Correct the two hidden 1B-encoder statements, DCB historical-residual terminology, and local-only source links in a separate content-only slide pass.
\item \textbf{Final gates.} The clean copy is free of notes, not a certificate of submission readiness. Author/advisor acceptance, figure permissions as applicable, final format review, committee corrections and required signatures/clearance remain open. No complete retraction-registry clearance is claimed.
\end{enumerate}
