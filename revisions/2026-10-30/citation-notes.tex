\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{October 30 Citation Consistency Review}
This addendum applies Dr. Moradi's citation guidance to the thesis only. It supersedes identifier-format recommendations in the historical October 28 and 29 notes. Publication identities and verified bibliographic metadata are retained.
\begin{itemize}
\item DOIs are omitted consistently because the published-paper set does not have complete verified DOI coverage. Known identifiers and source links remain in the verification ledger, outside the submission bibliography.
\item Published papers use their conference or journal records without duplicate preprint or web links. Dataset-description papers remain scholarly paper references; software/model releases retain URLs.
\item ROAD-Waymo (arXiv:2411.01683) and the ROAD++ challenge report (arXiv:2410.23077) retain arXiv identifiers. No formal publication record was established in the checks; an arXiv-issued DOI is not evidence of conference or journal publication.
\item Ultralytics explicitly states that it has not published a formal YOLOv8 research paper. The citation therefore identifies its software repository and recommended authors. The methods text now explicitly states that the Ultralytics implementation was used. No unverified software version is asserted.
\item ECCV entries use a consistent full conference name. InternVideo2 retains the publisher's citation year 2025 and explicitly identifies the ECCV 2024 proceedings. ICLR is spelled out consistently. Historical IEEE versus IEEE/CVF conference names are retained by edition rather than assigning a later sponsorship name to older papers.
\item All seven journal articles have page ranges or an article identifier. FRCB's 114489 is identified as an article number rather than a page number. Verified conference page ranges are retained; the advisor does not require their removal.
\end{itemize}
